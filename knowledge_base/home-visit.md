title: Home Visit Eligibility
source: home-visit.md
---

Home visits are eligible for Pediatrics and General Medicine patients who cannot travel and who live within ten kilometres of the clinic. A home visit must be booked at least twenty-four hours ahead and adds a three hundred INR travel charge on top of the specialty consultation fee. Cardiology, Dermatology, and Orthopedics visits are not eligible for home care under this rule. The travel charge is waived only when the clinic cancels the home visit, not when the patient cancels it.
