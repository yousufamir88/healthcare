source: emergency-protocol.md

If you may be facing a medical emergency, call local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. This assistant cannot diagnose chest pain, trouble breathing, severe bleeding, fainting, or any other urgent symptom. Clinic booking and this support channel are only for non-emergency policy questions and must not delay emergency care. After emergency treatment elsewhere, a routine clinic visit can be booked through the normal appointment process.
