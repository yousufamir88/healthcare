title: Follow-up Visit Discount Policy
source: follow-up-discount.md
---

A follow-up visit booked within fourteen days of a completed appointment in the same specialty receives a twenty percent discount on the consultation fee. The new appointment must be marked follow-up required for that discount to apply at billing. The twenty percent discount is not available when the earlier visit was cancelled or the patient did not attend. Cardiology follow-up visits use the same twenty percent discount on the cardiology consultation fee.
