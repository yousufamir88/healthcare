Published consultation list fees in this synthetic clinic are 600 INR for General Medicine, 750 INR for Pediatrics, 900 INR for Dermatology, 1300 INR for Orthopedics, and 1800 INR for Cardiology. An appointment record stores the fee quoted for that visit, drawn from the specialty band around the published list fee. The fee shown at booking is the amount collected when the visit is completed. These amounts are fabricated capstone examples for this support agent and are not a verified Practo price list.
