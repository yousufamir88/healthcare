title: Telemedicine Eligibility
source: telemedicine.md
---

Telemedicine video visits are available for General Medicine, Dermatology, and Pediatrics when the patient completed an in-person visit in the same specialty during the previous one hundred eighty days. First visits in Cardiology and first visits in Orthopedics are not eligible for a video slot. A video appointment uses the same cancellation window as an in-person clinic appointment. Telemedicine must not be used when the patient may need emergency services.
