title: Insurance Claim Process
source: insurance-claims.md
---

A patient may submit an insurance claim within fifteen days of a completed visit by providing the invoice and the appointment record id. The clinic prepares a claim-support note and does not guarantee that the insurer will approve payment. Visits that were cancelled or never attended are not eligible for a claim-support note. A cashless request must be flagged at booking and proceeds only when the insurer code on the request is accepted by the clinic desk.
