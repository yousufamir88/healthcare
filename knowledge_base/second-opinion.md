title: Second Opinion Process
source: second-opinion.md
---

A patient may request a second opinion within thirty days of a completed visit by asking for a different clinician in the same specialty. The second opinion is booked as a new appointment with a new record id and the specialty list fee. Notes from the first visit are shared only with the second clinician assigned to that new record. A second opinion is a scheduled clinic review and is not a substitute for emergency services.
