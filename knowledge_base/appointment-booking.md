title: Appointment Booking Policy
source: appointment-booking.md
---

A patient books a clinic appointment by selecting a specialty, a clinician, and an open slot at least four hours before the visit starts. Same-day General Medicine slots are released at 07:00 IST and remain bookable until that day's schedule is full. The confirmation message includes the appointment record id, the specialty, and the quoted consultation fee in INR. Walk-in requests are accepted only when that day's schedule still has an unused slot, and each booking creates one new record id rather than reusing an older visit.
