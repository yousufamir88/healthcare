title: Prescription Refill Policy
source: prescription-refills.md
---

A prescription refill can be requested after a completed visit when the original prescription is still within ninety days. The patient submits the refill request against the appointment record id, and a clinician reviews it before any medicine is released. Controlled medicines are never refilled through this support channel and always need a fresh clinician review in person. A refill does not add a new consultation fee unless the reviewing clinician requires a fresh visit.
