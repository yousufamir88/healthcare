title: Lab Test Turnaround Times
source: lab-turnaround.md
---

Routine blood-panel results are filed within twenty-four hours of sample collection at this synthetic clinic. Culture tests are filed within seventy-two hours of the sample reaching the lab bench. Orthopedics imaging reports are filed within forty-eight hours of the scan. The patient is notified when the result is attached to the appointment record id, and the support channel only reports these turnaround windows rather than interpreting the result.
