title: Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy
source: cancellation-rescheduling.md
---

A booked appointment may be cancelled or rescheduled until six hours before the scheduled start time. A cancellation made inside that window is stored with status Cancelled, and the consultation fee is not collected. Rescheduling keeps the original record id and sets the status to Rescheduled without opening a second record. A No-Show is recorded only when the patient does not arrive and did not cancel or reschedule before the six-hour window closed.
